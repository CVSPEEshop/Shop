About CVSPEE

CVSPEE is the official campus shop for Cavite State University, dedicated to providing students, teachers, and parents with everything they need for campus life — from official uniforms and department PE gear to school supplies, bags, and CVSU ID laces.

Our goal is to make it easy and convenient for the CVSU community to get authentic, good-quality campus essentials, all in one place.

FAQs & Help Center

How do I place an order?
Browse our products, pick a size and quantity, then check out through Buy Now or your Cart. You'll choose a payment method and submit payment confirmation to complete the order.

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept GCash and Cash on Delivery (COD).

How do I track my order?
Go to Account → Order to open Order Tracking. You'll see your orders grouped into To Ship, To Receive, To Review, and Returns.

How do I request a refund or return?
Once an order is delivered, open Order Tracking → To Review and tap Request Refund on the item. Fill out the form with your details, payment method for the refund, and reason, then submit. You can follow the status (Pending Approval → Returning → Success Return) under the Returns tab.

How do I leave a product review?
From Order Tracking → To Review, you can tap a quick star rating or Write Review for a full review with photos.

Do I need an account to order?
Yes, you'll need to sign in so we can keep your order history and let you track deliveries and returns.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
Message us as soon as possible through the order chat or our Contact page — we can still make changes while your order is pending approval.

Still need help?
Visit our Contact page and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Return & Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with your order. Here's how returns and refunds work at CVSPEE.

When can I request a return?
Once your order has been delivered, you can request a return or refund from Order Tracking → To Review by tapping Request Refund on the item.

What do I need to provide?
We'll ask for your full name, address, whether you're a teacher or student (with year level and section, if applicable), the payment method and account details we should send your refund to, your reason for the return, and a photo showing the issue.

What happens after I submit a request?
Your request goes to our team for review. You can follow its progress under Order Tracking → Returns, which moves through three stages: Pending Approval, Returning, and Success Return.

How long does approval take?
We review requests as quickly as we can. If we need more information, we'll reach out through your order chat.

Questions about a return?
Reach out through our Contact page and we'll be glad to help.

Data Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal information CVSPEE collects, why we collect it, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

What we collect
• Account details: your full name, username, email, password and role (student, teacher or parent), and your Student ID number if you are a student.
• ID verification: a photo of your ID and a selfie holding it.
• Orders: your name, phone number, pinned delivery location, payment confirmation screenshot and order history.
• Activity on the site: your cart, wishlist, order chat messages, and the reviews and photos you post.

Why we collect it
To create and verify your account, process and deliver your orders, answer your messages and refund requests, and keep the shop safe from fake accounts.

ID verification and AI
To check that your ID is real and matches the name and Student ID number you typed, your ID photo and selfie are sent through our server to an AI service (Google Gemini). The result of the check decides whether your verification passes. Messages you type in the shop's chat assistant are also processed by AI to prepare replies.

Where your information is stored
Your information is stored in our Firebase (Google Cloud) database. Your ID photo and selfie are saved with your account record so the verification can be reviewed by the shop team.

Who we share it with
We do not sell your information. We share it only with the services we need to run the site: Google Firebase for storage and Google Gemini for the AI checks described above.

How long we keep it
We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as it is needed for orders, refunds and verification. You can ask us to delete your account and information at any time.

Your rights
You have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access and correct it, to object to its processing, to ask for it to be deleted or blocked, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use any of these rights, contact us through our Contact page.

Keep your account safe
Please keep your password private and never share your verification codes. We will never ask for your card number, bank PIN or OTP.

How to Order

Ordering from CVSPEE takes a few simple steps.

  1. Create and verify your account. Sign up with your full name, username, email, password and role. Students and teachers use their official @cvsu.edu.ph email, and students also enter their Student ID number. Fill up the form first, then scan your ID, take a selfie holding it, and enter the verification code we send to your email.
  2. Find a product. Go to the Product page. Use the search box or Filters (category, gender and price) to narrow things down.
  3. Open the product. Tap a product to see its photos, details and size chart. Check the size chart to choose the right size.
  4. Choose your size and quantity. Then tap Add to Cart to keep shopping, or Buy to check out right away.
  5. Check out. Review your order. If you have a promo code, enter it here (it can't be used together with a product discount).
  6. Pay. Choose GCash or Cash on Delivery. For GCash, send the amount shown and keep a screenshot of your payment confirmation.
  7. Enter your delivery details. Give your full name and phone number, then pin your delivery location on the map.
  8. Track your order. Go to Account → Order to see your orders under To Ship, To Receive, To Review and Returns. You can message us through the order chat if you need to change something.
  9. Review or return. After delivery, you can rate the product. If there is a problem, see our Return & Refund Policy.

Need help? Visit our FAQs / Help Center or the Contact page.

Size Guide

Use these charts to choose your size. Each product page also shows its own size chart as soon as you open it.

How to measure
• Chest: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level.
• Waist: measure around your natural waistline.
• Foot length: stand on a sheet of paper and mark your heel and your longest toe, then measure the distance.

Tip: if you are between two sizes, check the chest and length numbers and pick the one that fits the way you like to wear it. Sizes may vary slightly between items.
